Ledger — Group Expense & Bill SplitterA zero-build, single-file group expense tracker and bill splitter built with HTML, Tailwind CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Ledger supports multi-trip workspaces, itemized receipt splitting, minimum-transfer settlements, category analytics, CSV exports, and printable group statements — all persisted locally in the browser with no backend required.FeaturesMulti-Trip & Group Workspaces: Manage separate groups and trips (preloaded with Weekend Outings, Goa Villa Trip, and Flatmates Monthly), create new workspaces on the fly, and switch seamlessly with automatic localStorage persistence.Four Split Modes:Equal Split: Divide the net bill evenly across checked participants.Receipt Items: Build an itemized receipt line by line, tap who shared each dish or item, auto-sum the subtotal, and split taxes/discounts proportionally.Exact Amounts: Enter custom per-person amounts with live unallocated or over-allocated difference alerts.By Shares (1x, 2x): Assign fractional or weighted shares per participant.Taxes, Discounts, Rounding & Currencies: Supports INR (₹), USD ($), EUR (€), and GBP (£), quick or custom tax/GST/service charge rates (0%, 5%, 10%, 15%, 18%, or custom), flat discounts, and per-person rounding (Exact (.00), Nearest 1, Round Up 10).Minimum-Transfer Settlement Engine: Calculates each person's net balance (gets + / owes − / settled), computes the minimum number of transfers required to clear all debts, and lets you log settled payments with one click (Record Paid).Participant Spend Matrix & Category Analytics: Compares Paid Up Front vs. Share Consumed vs. Net Position for every member alongside a color-coded category breakdown (Dining, Transport, Groceries, Utilities, Stay, Other) and Top Payer badge.Searchable Expense Ledger: Filter entries by category, search by description or payer, edit or remove logged expenses, and copy ready-to-share WhatsApp/UPI split summaries.Printable Statements & CSV Export: Generate an Official Group Statement modal with print/PDF styling (@media print) and one-click report copying, or download the workspace log as ledger-expenses.csv.Split Modes OverviewModeBest ForHow Tax & Discount ApplyEqual SplitShared cabs, stays, utilities, group mealsApplied to net subtotal, then divided evenly across selected membersReceipt ItemsRestaurant bills where people ordered different dishesDistributed proportionally based on each member's itemized subtotalExact AmountsFixed custom amounts per personLive delta
